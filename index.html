2026.08.29 [ORG]

Google Tasks → Todoist

Google Tasks is weak. No power. No one at Google uses it either. Todoist is the real tool.

How to migrate:

1. Get the tool
github.com/exploids/google-tasks-to-todoist — does the heavy lifting.

2. Export your data
Go to Google Takeout and download your tasks as JSON. Google gives you everything.

3. Convert JSON to CSV
Run the tool or convert manually. CSV is what Todoist actually understands.

4. Import to Todoist
Upload your CSV files per project. Todoist handles the rest. Clean. Organized. Works.

Google Tasks had its moment. It's over now.
